# Contributing to Cardboard++

First off, thank you for considering contributing to Cardboard++! It's people like you that make this project better for everyone.

Since this is a work-in-progress (and can get a bit chaotic), here are some ground rules to keep things moving in the right direction:

## Pull Request Requirements

Before you hit the submit button, make sure:

- **It actually compiles:** This sounds obvious, but please double-check that your changes don't break the build.
- **It works in real life:** Don't just trust the code; try it out on a device. The change needs to actually do what it's supposed to do in practice.
- **You explain what's going on:** In your PR description, tell me what you changed, how you tested it, and if there's anything I should watch out for.

## Testing Expectations

We value real-world verification:

- **Human testing is mandatory:** AI tools are great, but they don't know how VR feels on a phone strapped to your head, and most agents can't even test things like the SteamVR driver and Android app. Every change needs to be tested by a human.
- **Tell me how you tested it:** Just a quick note on what you did to verify the fix or feature.

## Stability and Documentation

Clear communication within the code helps future maintainers:

- **Warn the next person:** If you know your code has limitations or specific issues, leave a comment or mention it in the PR. It helps me (and anyone else) to find areas for improvement.
- **Be honest in your comments:** If a section of code is a "hacky workaround" or "needs a total rewrite," just say so! Honest comments are way better than perfect comments that nobody understands or excessively long.

## Use of AI Tools

I use AI too, so I'm not going to tell you not to. But let's not do it wrong:
- **Assisted, Not Automated:** AI assistance is allowed and encouraged for repetitive tasks, boilerplate code, or simple logic.
- **No "AI-only" PRs:** Please don't just let an AI agent generate a whole feature and ship it. They often produce unreliable results in complex projects like this. If you use AI, make sure *you* understand and have verified the changes.

Thank you for helping us bring the Meta Quest experience to Google Cardboard!
